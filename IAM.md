## Core Principles

1. **Ownership over familiarity:** If AI wrote it, I still owe the learning. If I cannot rebuild it closed-book, it does not count.
2. **Evidence over activity:** Commits, tests, reproducible containers, and working endpoints count. Passive tutorials, video hours, and unread agent PRs do not.
3. **Strict technical honesty:** Never validate broken logic or incomplete edge cases just to feel productive.
4. **One must-win outcome per day:** Finish, block, or deliberately stop one major outcome before opening another.

## What I Want to Prove

- I can build and defend a full-stack product with automated tests, migrations, and clean trust boundaries.
- I can design relational schemas, write queries, and explain transaction isolation and indexing tradeoffs.
- I can containerize applications with Docker, configure CI/CD pipelines, and manage secrets securely.
- I can independently derive core DSA patterns (sliding window, two pointers, hash maps, queues/stacks) in Python.
- I can walk an interviewer through my repository line-by-line without relying on AI to speak for me.

## Non-Goals

- Collecting 50 surface-level tutorials without building a real system.
- Grinding hundreds of LeetCode problems without understanding fundamental invariants.
- Adopting complex distributed systems (Kubernetes, microservices, Kafka) before mastering a clean monolithic architecture.
