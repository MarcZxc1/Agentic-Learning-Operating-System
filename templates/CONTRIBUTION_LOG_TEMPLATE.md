# Contribution Log

| Date | Outcome/change | Implemented by | My role | Review/integration evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use precise role labels:

- **Authored:** personally implemented the change.
- **Reviewed:** evaluated evidence/diff and requested or approved changes.
- **Designed/decided:** owned requirements, contract, architecture, or ADR.
- **Coordinated:** clarified ownership, dependency, risk, or deadline.
- **Integrated:** resolved contract/system issues and verified the combined path.

Do not claim teammates' implementation as personal coding. Link tasks, PRs, decisions, tests, demos, or meeting outcomes.
